About Refresh: Fetches publicly available Kalshi contract prices through a web proxy, with a public market-data fallback that can be delayed about 15 minutes. If a source is blocked, the page keeps the last displayed odds and marks that race as unavailable. The refresh does not change your checked scenarios, and probabilities are not counted votes.

How the math works: The starting caucus count is 47 Democratic-aligned seats (including two independents) and 53 Republican seats. Each Republican seat flipped adds one Democratic seat; each Democratic seat lost subtracts one. The scenario assumes all other races stay with their starting party.

Tie-breaker: At 50–50, Vice President JD Vance can break tie votes, so Republicans retain organizational control. An outright Democratic majority requires 51 seats.
